Period Definitions
Δ Day
vs. previous trading day's closing price.
WTD (Week-to-Date)
vs. the closing price of the weekend before this week - Sunday for 7-day-collected securities (FX/crypto/commodities), Friday for weekday-only securities.
MTD (Month-to-Date)
vs. the last available price of the prior calendar month (e.g. 31.07. as the reference for August) - not a rolling 30-day offset.
YTD (Year-to-Date)
vs. the last available price of the prior calendar year (31.12.) - not a rolling 365-day offset.
